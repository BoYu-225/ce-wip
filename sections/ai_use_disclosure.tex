\subsection*{AI use statement}

In this work, we used generative AI tools for figure illustration and polishment of the writing. We also used them to retrieve and summarize related works and modifying the training code. AI tools also helped us in early theoretical exploration. The ideation and evaluation were done by the authors. In paper writing we used AI tool to summarize and refine our drafts in theoretical exploration. We did not use generative AI tools for directly draft sections of the paper or generating synthetic datasets.
